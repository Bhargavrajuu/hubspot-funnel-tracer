# HubSpot Funnel Tracer: results

Built for HubSpot. Prepared by K Bhargav Raju.

## Key figures

- **Leads this quarter:** 6,000 (From 7 channels)
- **Lead -> customer:** 6.0% (363 customers)
- **Closed revenue:** $8.8M ($1.5K per lead)
- **Leakiest stage:** MQL -> SQL (Only 40.6% move on)
- **Faster follow-up worth:** +$11.8M (per year at a 4-hour target)

## Algorithm, as it ran

1. **Collect CRM touchpoints**: 6,000 leads, 17,972 touches
2. **Stitch journeys**: Average journey 3.0 touches
3. **Measure funnel stages**: Lead -> customer 6.0%
4. **Markov chain attribution**: Baseline conversion 6.05%; top removal effect Content Downloads
5. **Compare with last-touch**: Biggest last-touch gap: Partner Referrals
6. **Lead scoring model**: Strongest trait: ICP fit score
7. **Find the leaks**: Worst stage: MQL -> SQL at 40.6%
8. **LLM summary**: Ready

## Recommended actions

1. **Fix the MQL -> SQL handoff.** Only 40.6% of MQLs move on. Review lost MQLs with sales weekly and tighten what qualifies. *Impact: 1,817 lost*
2. **Call every MQL back within 4 hours.** Median follow-up is 11.1 hours and slow follow-up is one of the strongest negatives in the lead score. *Impact: +$11.8M/yr*
3. **Optimise LinkedIn Ads for pipeline, not form fills.** It brings 21% of first-touch leads but only $532 per touched lead. *Impact: Budget shift*
4. **Invest more in Partner Referrals and Webinars.** Highest revenue per touched lead and large Markov removal effects; last-touch reporting under-credits them. *Impact: Higher ROI*
5. **Send only 20+ point leads to sales.** They are 15% of leads and produced 31% of revenue. *Impact: Rep time*
